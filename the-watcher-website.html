01 / THE ECOSYSTEM

One watchful system.
Multiple layers of defence.

One bot, four layers. Switch on the protection your community needs and manage every layer from the same menu in Telegram.

01 / MODERATION
◈

Watcher Guard

Keep conversations healthy with spam rules you set yourself.

  • Banned phrases, even with odd capitals, spacing or styled letters
  • Block forwarded messages
  • Hide “joined” and “left” notices
  • Protect several groups from one account
  • Admins are never affected
02 / IDENTITY
◎

Identity Watch

Spot lookalike accounts and impersonation before trust is exploited.

  • Flags new members whose name looks like one you protect
  • Searches Telegram for fake channels, groups, accounts and bots
  • Reports each fake once, with a link and a score
  • Scans every 3 days (Standard) or every day (Premium), or on demand with one tap
  • Alerts only. It never bans anyone
03 / LINKS
⌁

Link Sentinel

Identify suspicious links and phishing attempts shared in chat.

  • Flags shorteners, lookalike domains and punycode tricks
  • Catches links hidden behind “click here”
  • Rechecks messages that are edited later
  • Optional auto-delete for suspicious links
  • Block all links, with an allowed list for your own sites
04 / GROUP SAFETY
⌖

Raid Shield

Stop brand-new accounts from flooding or spamming your group.

  • New-member lockdown: no links or @mentions for 1 hour to 7 days
  • Anti-flood for anyone over 10 messages a minute
  • Works on photos and stickers too
  • Admins are never affected

05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Will it delete my admins’ messages?

Never. Admins are exempt from every deletion rule. If an admin posts a suspicious link you still get an alert, in case their account was hijacked.

Where do alerts go?

To your private chat with the bot, never into the group. Press Start once so Telegram lets the bot message you.

Does it ban people automatically?

No. It deletes messages, and for lookalike joiners it only alerts you, so a real member is never banned by mistake.

Can it remove a fake channel?

It finds and reports them to you. Only Telegram can take one down, so use the Report button in Telegram or the form at telegram.org/abuse.

How often does it look for fakes?

Standard scans automatically every three days and Premium every day, and you can also tap the scan button yourself once per scan period. Basic does not include scans, but it still alerts you when a lookalike joins your group. You are only told about each fake once.

Does it protect channels?

Only admins can post in a channel, so spam appears in its linked discussion group, where comments live. Register that group and every comment is covered.

What happens when the trial ends?

Protection switches off and your settings are kept. You get a reminder two days before, and a message in the bot with the plans so you can subscribe and switch it back on. Your settings stay saved if you move between plans.

How much does it cost?

After the 7-day free trial, Basic is $9 a month, Standard is $15 a month and Premium is $19 a month. There is no free plan after the trial. Card and bank payments are charged in naira.

How do I pay?

Inside the Telegram bot. Pick a plan, then choose Telegram Stars, either as an auto-renewing monthly subscription or a single 30-day payment, or pay by card, bank transfer or USSD on Paystack’s secure page. Card and bank payments are a single payment for 30 days, and you get a reminder before it ends.

What does it store about my group?

Your settings, protected names and group ID, plus member join times for 14 days if you use the new-member lockdown. It does not keep a copy of your chat history.
